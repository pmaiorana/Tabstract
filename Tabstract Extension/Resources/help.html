How to use Tabstract

Getting Started

Tabstract helps you save and organize your Safari tabs. To get started, right-click the Safari toolbar, select Customize, and drag the Tabstract icon to your toolbar. Once installed in your toolbar, click Tabstract, then Save Tabs to capture all open tabs in the current window.

If your computer supports Apple Intelligence, Tabstract can automatically categorize your saved tabs into different topic-based groups. Click the ✨ sparkle icon next to any session title to generate a new title. If your computer doesn't support Apple Intelligence tab sessions will be named with the date and time.

You can copy links from other applications and paste those URLs directly into Tabstract! Tabstract will automatically fetch the page titles and add them to your collection.

The toolbar badge displays the number of saved sessions, so they're not forgotten about even though they're out-of-sight. Snooze hides the toolbar badge for up to 24 hours, or you can disable it altogether in settings.

Hold Shift while clicking any session's collapse button to expand or collapse all sessions at once.

Click and drag individual links, and sessions, to rearrange your saved tabs into any order you prefer.

Deleted links are moved to trash for 30 days before they are then automatically deleted. Trash is limited to 1,000 items to help keep Tabstract fast.

Tabstract includes keyboard shortcuts and modifier key combinations for power users. Shortcuts can be disabled in Settings.

View all shortcuts
Main shortcuts:

Save all tabs: ⌘ Cmd + ⇧ Shift + S
Save current tab only: ⌘ Cmd + ⇧ Shift + D
Open saved sessions list: ⌘ Cmd + ⇧ Shift + H

Quick actions:

Focus search box: /
Create new session: N

Modifier key combinations:

Save only current tab: ⇧ Shift + Click Save Tabs
Expand/collapse all sessions: ⇧ Shift + Click session collapse button
Temporarily reverse "Remove tabs after restoring" setting: ⇧ Shift + Click Restore
Temporarily reverse "Restore tabs in background" setting: ⌥ Option + Click Restore

Backup and Restore

Last backup: —
Tabstract automatically backs up your data every time you save tabs and once per hour. Backups older than 24 hours are thinned to one per day, then one per week, and removed after 30 days. Manual backups are kept indefinitely.
